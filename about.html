About Lattie's Decor & Events

"We bring elegance, thoughtfulness, and artistry to every single detail. Our team is passionate about creating magical settings for the moments that matter most."

Our Story

Lattie's Decor & Events began with a simple belief: every celebration deserves to feel special. From the joy of a wedding to the warmth of a baby shower, the fun of a birthday or the dignified tribute of a memorial service, we style spaces that reflect the people being honoured. With studios in Midrand and Sandton, we serve clients across Gauteng and travel nationwide.

Our Promise

We promise three things at every event: elegance in the look, thoughtfulness in the planning, and artistry in every detail. If something is not right on the day, our team fixes it on the spot.

How We Work

  1. Consultation: tell us about your event through our enquiry form.
  2. Design: we propose a theme, colour palette and layout for your venue.
  3. Setup: our team delivers and installs everything before your guests arrive.
  4. Celebrate: you enjoy the day while we handle the pack-down.
